## Hi, I am Abhi

Thank you for coming to my blog. I am really glad you are here.

I have been fortunate to have a career in energy. The way we produce and use energy is changing fast, and I get to learn something new almost every week. I enjoy that a lot.

I started this blog to clear my thoughts. Writing things down helps me think, and I would like to share what I learn with you. Some posts will be short notes, some will have charts, and some will just be ideas I am still working through.

I owe a lot to Mark Mikofski, Rounak Kharait and Paul Wolffersdorff. They have taught me so much and supported me along the way. Thank you.

If you have any thoughts on what I write, please reach out. I would be happy to hear from you.

Abhi
